04 Website On-Page & Schema Technical Audit

Evaluation of zepservices.com on-page signals that feed Google's local ranking algorithms.

On-Page Architecture & Metadata

  • Page Title Tags: Good brand inclusion, but lacks primary service intent hierarchy on sub-pages.
  • Meta Descriptions: Several service pages have duplicate or missing meta descriptions, hurting click-through rates.
  • H1 & Heading Hierarchy: Multiple H1 tags detected on core service pages causing keyword dilution.
  • URL Silos: Potential cannibalization between generic cleaning and specialized air duct service URLs.

Structured Schema & NAP Consistency

  • LocalBusiness Schema: Basic schema present, but lacks multi-location parent/child department nesting.
  • HVAC & Cleaning Schema: Missing specialized HVACBusiness and DryCleaningOrLaundry structured types.
  • NAP Consistency: Minor formatting discrepancies in phone/address formatting across footer vs. location landing pages.
  • Geo-Coordinates: Missing precise latitude/longitude tags in schema to anchor Google Maps signals.
