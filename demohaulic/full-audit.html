3 demohaulic.com: what's wrong with the website

Google currently shows this website for zero searches and sends it zero visitors a month, three months running. Here's why, page by page.

Every page tells Google it's a copy of the homepage.

Each page carries a canonical tag pointing at https://demohaulic.com. That's an instruction to ignore the page and index the homepage instead. Your junk removal page, your bathroom demolition page and your quote pages are all effectively excluded from search.

Evidence: rel="canonical" href="https://demohaulic.com" on /junk-removal, /bathroom-demolition, /free-quote, /demo-quote, /home-1760, /home-1776.

Every page has the same title and description, and it's about bathroom tile.

The title tag is what shows up as the blue link in Google. All seven pages we checked, including the junk removal page, use the identical title and description, and neither mentions junk removal, hauling or Eastpointe.

Evidence: title on every page is "Bathroom Demo & Tile Removal - Demohaulic Macomb County Michigan". Description on every page begins "Bathroom demo and tile removal in Macomb County…"

16 H1 headings on the homepage. A page should have one.

The H1 is the headline Google uses to understand what a page is about. The homepage has sixteen of them, and they include a phone number, an email line, the word "STOP…" and bullet points. The junk removal page has ten. To Google that reads as a page with no subject.

Evidence, homepage H1s include: "Call / Text: 586-202-4056", "Email: …", "STOP…", "Stop doing yOUR OWN bathroom DEMO.", "• We protect the house.", "• We contain the dust."

Four copies of the homepage are live at different addresses.

/home-1760, /home-1776, /demo-quote and /free-quote all serve the same page as the homepage, word for word. Duplicate pages split whatever authority the site has and confuse which one Google should show.

Evidence: identical title, description, headings and word count (about 400 words) on all five URLs. /privacy and /privacy-policy, and /terms and /terms-and-conditions, are also duplicates.

The words customers search for barely appear.

Across the whole site, "Eastpointe" appears on zero pages. "Junk removal" appears once on the homepage. "Demolition" appears once. Each page has roughly 400 words of visible text. Google can't rank a page for a service the page doesn't name.

Nearly every image is missing alt text.

Six of the seven images on each page have no alt text, which is how Google reads what a picture shows. The images are also larger than 100 KB and not in a modern format, which slows the page on phones.

The phone number isn't tappable.

The number is written into a heading as text, not as a link. On a phone, a customer has to copy it out by hand. There are no tel: links anywhere on the pages we checked.

Redirect and orphan clutter.

Of 22 URLs Google knows about, 10 redirect somewhere else, one returns a 404, and 6 are orphans no other page links to. robots.txt is empty, the crawl flagged the sitemap, and the site sends no security policy header.

Evidence: /blog, /contact-us, /cost-calculators, /get-a-quote, /home, /schedule-pickup all redirect; /cdn-cgi/l/email-protection returns 404.

Missing social and structured data on most pages.

No og:url, no Twitter title or description, and the Open Graph title runs over 60 characters. Business schema markup exists on the homepage and quote pages but not on the bathroom demolition or junk removal pages, which are the ones that should carry it.

172 backlinks, and almost all of them are junk.

The site has links from 122 other domains. They are overwhelmingly link farms and unrelated foreign sites: casino and poker pages, "guest post" networks, music download sites, each linking from an address like /all/799/14.html. Links like these don't help, and a pile of them can hurt. They should be disavowed.

Examples: bestnz-poker-casinoslot.com, ufabettererm4.com, chordmp3.net, plrdownloadshub.com, firstguestpost.com, backlinkocean.com.

What's fine.

Pages load with a 200 status, the homepage carries business schema, and the site is on a modern host. The bones are usable; the content and settings on top of them are working against you.
